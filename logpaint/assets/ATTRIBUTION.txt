Project: Escher and the Droste effect, Universiteit Leiden.
The source page credits the periodic grayscale reconstruction to Jacqueline
Hofstra. The project also credits Hans Richter, Joost Batenburg, Bart de Smit
and Hendrik Lenstra. It is based on M. C. Escher's Print Gallery (1956).
The project website states that M. C. Escher works are copyright Cordon
Art-Baarn-the Netherlands, used there by permission, all rights reserved.
This attribution does not assert that the artwork is openly licensed.

Paper: Bart de Smit and Hendrik W. Lenstra Jr., The Mathematical Structure
of Escher's Print Gallery, Notices of the AMS 50(4), April 2003, 446–451.
https://www.ams.org/notices/200304/fea-escher.pdf

LogPaint reference asset ID: leiden-p4-v1. The published web JPEG is low
resolution. A single approximate rectangular log period is sampled from it
at runtime (x=50, y=38.75, width=310*log(256)/(2*pi), height=310), using q=256.
This fixes the default scale/orientation; small JPEG/crop seam errors remain.
It is a derived preview, not a claim of pixel-identical historical restoration.
